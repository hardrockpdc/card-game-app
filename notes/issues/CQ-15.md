## Problem

Surfaced during the Phase 1.3 sweep of `archive/PROJECT_NOTES.md`'s "Next Steps When We
Resume" section (~line 480), not present in the original tracker under its own ID.

Original archived text: "Replace silent sound WAV placeholders with real audio —
DEFERRED (sound menu item temporarily removed; will revisit when real audio is
sourced)." A related note (~line 600) records that the mute-toggle menu item was
deliberately removed from all 9 game screens on 2026-05-13 specifically because "no
actual gameplay audio is hooked up yet... a toggle that toggles nothing is confusing" —
`game/sounds.js` infrastructure was kept intact for future use.

## Verified 2026-08-15

The archive's premise is stale — real audio playback was built at some point since,
contrary to what the doc still says. But the result is a different, more specific gap
than "no audio exists," worth tracking on its own.

`game/sounds.js` now does real playback, not just mute-flag bookkeeping: it imports
`createAudioPlayer`/`setAudioModeAsync` from `expo-audio` (line 1), `require()`s 4 real
WAV files (lines 5-8), builds a player pool in `initSounds()` (lines 21-43, invoked at
app startup, `App.js:113`), and `playSound(name)` actually calls `player.seekTo(0);
player.play()` (lines 58-69). The WAV files are confirmed non-silent (8-bit/8000Hz mono
PCM with real sample data) — but all four (`card_flip.wav`, `card_deal.wav`, `win.wav`,
`error.wav`) are byte-for-byte identical (same MD5, 1644 bytes). One generic beep is
reused for every event; the "will revisit when real audio is sourced" half of the
original deferral is only partly done.

**The mute/sound toggle is back in the menu on 8 of 9 game screens** — `GameMenu.js`
unconditionally renders a Sound row regardless of what a screen passes in, wired via
`GameHeader` (Blackjack, Go Fish, Poker, Rummy, Last Card, Who Am I, Conquián) and
`GameMenuButton` (Solitaire). But **actual `playSound()` call sites are narrow**: only
`screens/GameScreen.js` (Blackjack — win/card_deal/card_flip) and `components/Toast.js`
(an error sound, only reachable from `screens/RummyGameScreen.js`, the one screen that
renders `<Toast>`). The other 6 game screens (Go Fish, Poker, Last Card, Who Am I,
Conquián, Solitaire) show a fully working-looking mute toggle that controls audio that
never plays there — a toggle for nothing, on two-thirds of the app's game screens.
`MemoryGameScreen.js` has no hamburger menu at all (no `GameHeader`/`GameMenu` import),
so it has neither the toggle nor any sound calls — a separate, narrower gap.

## Fix sketch

1. Source or generate 3-4 actually distinct short SFX to replace the identical-MD5 beep
   reused for card_flip/card_deal/win/error — or explicitly decide the shared placeholder
   is acceptable long-term.
2. Add `playSound(...)` calls to the 6 screens that currently show a working mute toggle
   but never call it (Go Fish, Poker, Rummy dealing/win events, Last Card, Who Am I,
   Conquián, Solitaire) — or, if not prioritized soon, hide the toggle on screens with no
   sound calls so it doesn't imply a feature that isn't there.
3. Separately decide whether Memory Match should get a `GameHeader`/menu at all — smaller,
   unrelated gap.
