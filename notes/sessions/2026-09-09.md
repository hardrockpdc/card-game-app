## What was verified

All six online games hosted on emulator-5554 and joined from emulator-5556:

- **Poker** — the important one, since 2026-09-08 rewrote its deal, phase machine and
  showdown and only tested single-player. Header read Texas Hold'em, flop/turn/river
  dealt over the network, and the showdown scored a **two-Queen board** correctly:
  client 5♠6♦ Two Pair over host A♣J♠ One Pair, 40 pot paid right. Both hand labels came
  from the shared `getPokerHandLabel`, so the evaluator swap holds over the wire.
- **Conquián** — stock 20 off the 40-card deck, ten valid Spanish-deck cards each, and a
  pass genuinely crossed devices: host's 2♣ left and the client's J♥ arrived.
- **Rummy** — stock 31, deadwood 64 and 65 both exact sums, no card in both hands.
- **Last Card** — draw 93, and playability correct against an orange 1 (orange 9 by
  colour, green 1 by rank, everything else dimmed).
- **Go Fish** and **Who Am I** were covered 2026-09-08.

**Hand privacy held in every game.** Each client saw only its own cards; opponents
rendered a count or nothing at all, never faces, until showdown where a showdown exists.
That is what [[LAUNCH-3]] needed and it is why that ticket moved.

Incidental: the daily-bonus streak advanced to Day 2 / 125 overnight, so that logic works
across a real date change.

## Decisions

- **Recorded the retest with its limits rather than as a clean pass.** Two emulators on
  one machine are separate installs authenticating independently, which is the property a
  Firebase rules check needs — but they are not two physical handsets, and the note says
  so. Nothing in the repo can read the console back either, so this is evidence of correct
  behaviour, not a transcript of the rules.
- **Stopped chaining blind through multi-screen navigation.** Two Conquián attempts were
  lost to a mistapped dialog button cascading into a disconnect. Verifying the lobby
  reached 2/N before pressing Start cost one screenshot and removed the whole class of
  failure.
