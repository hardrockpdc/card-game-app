## Decisions

- **Emulators over the phone.** The dev-build APK is universal (`lib/x86_64/`), so it was
  pulled off the device and installed on two x86_64 AVDs rather than rebuilt. Two
  emulators also made the online multiplayer tests possible. Local-network multiplayer
  still needs real devices — emulators can't see each other over mDNS.
- **Working copy moved off the network share.** Metro never finished a bundle from
  `\10.0.0.1\...`; from a local disk it bundles in ~28s and the test suite dropped from
  518s to 4s. Same root cause as [[BUG-13]].
- **Poker: implement rather than hide.** Three variants were non-functional. Options were
  hide them, do Omaha only, or build all three; Pedro chose all three. The estimate was
  wrong in [[BUG-11]]'s favour — `game/poker.js` already had the evaluator, the draw
  helpers and the AI discard chooser, all tested and imported by nothing. Most of the work
  was wiring, not writing.
- **Stud redaction lives in `game/poker.js`, not the screen.** `toPublic` strips `hands`
  and must keep doing so; code that can leak hole cards should be code that can be tested.
- **Fixing the Back handler's scope alone was not enough.** Verified the hard way: with the
  handler focus-scoped but navigation untouched, Back stopped being swallowed and instead
  dropped the player back into the room they had just quit. Recorded in [[BUG-12]].
